# Cover Letter

**Date**: August 16, 2026

**To the Editor**,

Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "**IL-MLIP-Benchmark: A Benchmark
Dataset and Energy-Decomposition Analysis for Ionic Liquid Machine-Learned
Interatomic Potentials**" for consideration.

**Significance and novelty.** Machine-learned interatomic potentials (MLIPs) have
revolutionized molecular and catalytic simulation, yet ionic liquids (ILs) — a
prominent class of electrolytes for batteries and catalysis — lack any systematic
MLIP benchmark. Our work contributes two things:

1. **IL-MLIP-Benchmark**, the first benchmark dataset of IL cation–anion pairs
   (435 conformations across 8 IL combinations with energies and forces), filling
   the IL gap analogous to OC20 for catalysis.
2. **A quantitative energy-decomposition analysis** showing isolated ions are
   energetically rigid (E_cation 54 meV, E_anion 14 meV), while the interaction
   term carries the full conformational dependence — a clean physical insight
   for IL MLIP design.

**Key results.** MACE fine-tuning achieves chemical accuracy (23.3 meV/atom,
5-fold CV), a 263× improvement over from-scratch SchNet, and generalizes across
unseen ILs (mean 37.6 meV/atom). These establish that pretraining priors are
necessary for IL MLIPs.

The dataset and code are publicly available (Zenodo DOI pending; GitHub
linfuxing123/IL-Property-ML).

We believe this work fits the scope of your journal's data-driven chemistry /
methodology sections. All authors have approved the submission.
